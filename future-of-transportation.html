Designing the Future of Transportation

A multi-module journey where students explore sustainable energy and autonomous technology to design, develop, and pitch a nature-inspired, futuristic transportation concept.

Program Overview

This program takes students on a journey to invent the future. Learners dive into the technologies shaping our world, from renewable energy to self-driving vehicles, and use their knowledge to create and pitch an innovative transportation solution inspired by the natural world.

Key Learning Modules

1. Exploring the Depths, Heights, and Beyond

Students gain a deep appreciation for sustainable energy, understand technological advancements in transportation, and imagine what the future of mobility might hold.

2. Navigating Tomorrow: The Era of Autonomous Vessels

A forward-thinking module exploring the world of autonomous navigation. Students delve into self-controlled vehicles, from drones and cars to ships, learning how they perceive and navigate the world.

3. From Concept to Pitch

The concluding module where learners select one form of transportation to develop. They are guided through concept development, testing, refinement, and finally, presenting their innovation to potential investors.
