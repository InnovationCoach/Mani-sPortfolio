InnoVision: Empower Your Ideas

A multi-phase program where students select a specialized Innovation Pathway and collaborate across disciplines to solve real-world challenges using the Design Thinking process.

Duration

14 Weeks

Core Focus

Interdisciplinary Design

Final Outcome

Innovation Showcase

Program Overview: Choose Your Mission

In this program, students choose one of four Innovation Pathways. Each one represents a key part of solving real-world problems. They gain foundational skills, complete hands-on design challenges, and collaborate across pathways to build future-ready, sustainable community solutions.

"Collaborate. Design for Impact."

The Learning Phases

Phase 1: Passion-Driven Knowledge Exploration (Weeks 1-4)

Students engage in hands-on activities, research, and discussions to build foundational knowledge in their chosen pathway, culminating in a "Passion Profile Presentation" to synthesize their insights.

Phase 2: Interdisciplinary Design & Innovation (Weeks 5-14)

Students from different pathways converge into interdisciplinary teams. They use the Design Thinking process (Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, Test) to solve real-world challenges and develop physical and digital prototypes.

The Four Innovation Pathways

Technology in Medical & Sport Science

Biometrics, rehabilitation tech, and human performance sensors.

Architecture & Climate Adaptation

Resilient urban spaces, passive cooling, and sustainable materials.

Storytelling through Music & Animation

Digital media synthesis, immersive audio, and communicative arts.

Sustainable Fashion Design

Circular textiles, biodegradable materials, and ethical manufacturing.
