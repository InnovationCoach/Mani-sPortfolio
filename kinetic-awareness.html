Kinetic Awareness — Moving Art for Change

A 9-week program where students explore the physics of motion, culminating in a public art event to raise environmental awareness and build interactive kinetic sculptures.

Duration

9 Weeks

Core Focus

Physics & Art

Final Outcome

Kinetic Sculpture

Program Philosophy

This program is more than a checklist—it’s a personal power-up guide for students to become stronger thinkers, collaborators, and change-makers. In this project, they’re not just completing tasks; they are becoming strategic thinkers, innovative designers, and culturally aware, adaptable leaders.

"The skills you are developing aren’t just for this project — they’re life skills that will make you future-ready for any challenge, career, or collaboration."

The Learning Journey

The 9-week program is divided into two main phases. In the first four weeks, learners explore the physics of motion through hands-on activities. They learn to use the scientific process as a tool to gain a better understanding of the connection between Mathematics and the principles of motion in Physics.

The program culminates in a student-organized event to raise awareness about an environmental issue. Each group of learners uses their understanding to build an interactive kinetic sculpture that demonstrates their concerns through a blend of art, physics, and creativity.
